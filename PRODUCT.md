# Picky

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A quick, shared-phone chooser for deciding who does something, plus simple dice and number picks. Open quickly and play without an account or network after the first installation.

## Users and Operating Context

People making quick decisions together around one phone. Finger 5+ lets larger groups register one person at a time without keeping every finger on the display.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Eleven modes: Finger, Wheel, 4-Way, Dice, 1–100, Multi Dice, Finger 5+, Teams, Teams 5+, Ranking, Tournament 5+. More opens the full list and Settings. Users can pin up to three modes; Finger and Wheel are the defaults. Appearance and pins persist; game-specific settings reset on mode changes and app reopening, while finger release preserves them. Finger 5+ supports choosing several winners, defaulting to one.

Static offline PWA with local scripts and assets. Dice must be simple Blender-authored models, inexpensive to load. Dice and number games start after a 2.4-second pulsing hold, with a fast animation and a large result on a colored background.

Minimalism follows Finger mode: hold through the selection and result, then lift to clear. Dice and number results contain only the outcome, without replay buttons or explanatory captions. Finger 5+ starts with one tap on Pick, highlights winning bubbles at their original positions for two seconds, and keeps its roster between selections. Wheel automatically counts held fingers. A single current-mode button opens a popup of other pins and More; the full mode list is centered and scrollable, and Settings uses a compact two-column pin grid.

Teams balances people into 2–5 teams, at most four people each, connected by lines. Teams 5+ uses tap registration like Finger 5+. Ranking puts distinct ranks directly on up to five held finger dots. Team and ranking results remain readable for 1.5 seconds after release. Tournament 5+ randomly advances one winner from each small group into a winners bracket, for 6–25 people total; there is no separate regular tournament or manual match tracking.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the dark Picky identity. Offer a comprehensive cream, illustrated cartoon light theme using the supplied red-accent wordmark. Every game, result, control, and menu participates in the selected theme. Favor distinct player colors, especially on the wheel.

Phone home-screen icons use an opaque pure-black background behind the existing mark. Mobile browser visits begin with a concise installation guide tailored to iPhone/iPad or Android, with a quiet “Try in browser” option for the current visit. Repeat the guide on browser returns; do not persist a skip preference. Installed apps and desktop browsers open directly to the game.
