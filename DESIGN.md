# Picky interface

This extends the existing game surface. The app is a compact tool, with the play area taking priority over navigation. Keep the center uncluttered, controls at the top, and a single current-mode pill at the bottom. The pill opens a popup of other pinned modes plus More. More opens a scrolling, centered column of icons above mode names. Settings uses an inline appearance switch and a compact two-column pin grid, following the supplied references.

Finger 5+ bubbles appear immediately at each person's touch position and remain there after release. Preserve this free placement; do not arrange players into a grid or list.

Dice, Multi Dice, number picks, and Finger 5+ retain a small Picky wordmark above the play area. Dice and number results show only the large outcome on its colored background. Finger 5+ highlights winning bubbles at their original positions, with the winning colors filling the background. No replay buttons, roll breakdowns, player counts, range captions, or navigation. Finger mode is the reference for winner highlights. Release preserves settings; Finger 5+ returns to the same roster automatically after a two-second result.

All buildups use the same three pulses over 2.4 seconds. Only the game pieces remain visible: hide branding, controls, navigation, and hints during the buildup. Do not introduce progress bars or charging text. Finger 5+ registers players immediately; one tap on Pick starts its pulsing selection without needing a held contact. For held modes, releasing during a buildup or roll cancels it without leaving a delayed result. Wheel counts held fingers automatically and uses a large wheel above them.

Dark keeps a black background, cool blue controls, the original gradient wordmark, and separated luminous player colors. Light uses warm cream, dark brown contours, red active controls, an illustrated wordmark, modest tactile edges, and solid player colors. The supplied logos define each identity.

Dice use tiny Blender mesh data rendered with perspective and shading. They are still at rest, jump and spin during a short roll, land on the correct visible face, and give way to a large numeric result. Numbers use elapsed-time animation so low and high outcomes take equally long.

Respect reduced motion, visible keyboard focus, safe areas, and narrow phone layouts. Theme colors must apply to menus, settings, controls, dice, fingers, wheels, dividers, roster numbers, and results. Outcome text chooses a readable foreground for its background.

The mobile install guide uses the same quiet branding and theme, with brief platform-specific Home Screen steps. Keep “Try in browser” visually secondary, readable, and easy to tap. The guide repeats on mobile browser visits and returns, with no saved dismissal; it never appears in an installed app or on desktop. Home-screen icon artwork stays on a fully opaque black square so the operating system supplies its own corner mask.

Teams and Ranking extend the original finger surface. Teams use shared colors and a few connecting lines; Ranking places the rank inside each dot. Keep those results in place for 1.5 seconds after everyone lifts. Teams 5+ keeps the exact tap positions and numbered roster from the existing 5+ interaction.

Tournament 5+ is an automatic chooser. Between batches, show only the round name and required finger count; the final stage is explicitly “Winners bracket.” During selection, only unnumbered finger dots remain. The winner fills the background with their color and pulses in place, like Finger mode. Round changes and reset happen automatically after release and the short reading pause. Do not add ranks, a bracket dashboard, matchup tracking, next buttons, replay buttons, or result captions.
